From Abhinav Rathi

Gunnu aaj naye saal par main aap se dil ki baat kehna chahta hoon aap meri zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat hissa ho aap ke bina har khushi adhoori lagti hai

Aap jis pyaar se mujhe samajhti ho jis tarah har haal mein mera saath deti ho wahi baat mujhe har din aap se aur zyada pyaar karna sikhaati hai

Main chahta hoon ke aap hamesha apne aap par bharosa rakho kyunki aap jaisi ho wahi meri duniya ki sabse badi taqat hai

Aap ke saath har sapna sach lagta hai aur har mushkil aasaan ho jaati hai kyunki aap mere saath ho

Main vaada karta hoon ke main hamesha aap ki izzat karunga aap ki khushi ko apni pehli zimmedari banaunga

Aap meri zindagi ki woh roshni ho jo har andhere ko khatam kar deti hai

Main aap ke saath budhe hona chahta hoon aap ke saath har saal aur zyada pyaar mein padna chahta hoon

Aap ke bina main apne future ki kalpana bhi nahi kar sakta

Aap meri har jeet ka sabab ho aur meri har haar ka sahara ho

Har naya saal mujhe sirf ek hi cheez aur zyada mehsoos karata hai ke main aap se bepanah pyaar karta hoon

Gunnu aap meri zindagi ki kahani ho jisme har page par sirf mohabbat likhi hai

Main chahta hoon ke hum dono ka pyaar waqt ke saath aur gehra hota jaaye

Yeh pal meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat yaad hai

Aap ke saath har tasveer ek kahani sunati hai

Aap meri duniya ho meri jaan ho

Aap ke bina kuch bhi poora nahi lagta

Forever us always

I Promise 💍
